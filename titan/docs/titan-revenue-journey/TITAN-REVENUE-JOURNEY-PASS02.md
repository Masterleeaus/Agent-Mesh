# Titan Revenue Journey — Pass 02

Pass 02 adds an evidence envelope around the retained lead/opportunity lifecycle engine. It records the canonical CRM source, explicit commercial ownership snapshot, lifecycle transition evidence and correlation provenance without creating or mutating CRM entities.

The lifecycle engine remains the transition validator. The new envelope fails closed on source-domain drift, missing ownership evidence, company-boundary violations and illegal transitions. Ownership evidence is descriptive/auditable only: identity and ownership metadata do not grant execution authority.
