How Dassi works

You tell Dassi the outcome you want — not a list of clicks. It then does the work for you: reading the page, working out the steps, clicking and filling things in, one action at a time.

When something doesn't go as expected, it notices and adjusts instead of giving up — so the whole job actually gets finished, across your tabs and on the accounts you're already signed into.

Dassi runs an agent loop: read the page → choose a tool → act → check the result → adjust — repeating until the job is done. That feedback loop is how it works through a real workflow and recovers when a step fails, rather than replaying fixed clicks.

It drives the browser with 20+ tools, for example:

  • navigate, click & fill (element_interact)
  • read page text, take screenshots
  • run JavaScript, manage tabs
  • invoke reusable skills

The result: it does the work end to end — in your browser, on the accounts you're already signed into.

Turn a workflow into a skill

Work with Dassi until a task is right, save it as a reusable skill, then trigger it later just by asking — and keep refining it as your process changes.

  1. 1
    Describe it in plain language
    "Every Monday, pull last week's signups from our dashboard, summarize them, and post it to #growth on Slack." Dassi works out the steps and runs them.
  2. 2
    Review & fine-tune together
    Watch how it went and adjust with it — "also flag churned accounts", "make the summary shorter" — until it's exactly right.
  3. 3
    Save it as a skill
    Once you're happy, tell Dassi to save the workflow as a reusable, named skill.
  4. 4
    Trigger it anytime — just ask
    Run it again with plain language, no rigid commands. Need a change later? Just tell it, and the skill keeps improving.

Give it your files

Add a reference — a file or folder on your computer — and Dassi can read it while it works. Add it once; it stays available across all your conversations.

Open Settings → References and click + File or + Folder — or just drag a folder into the side panel. Then pick which references to share in each chat with the folder button in the composer.

Let Dassi watch on a schedule

Ask it to do something every day at a set time on a site. It runs even when the panel is closed and pings you with a desktop notification when it kicks off — so it quietly keeps an eye on things for you.

Just describe it in the side panel, e.g. "Every day at 8:30, open Gmail and summarize my unread emails." Approve the consent card once, and Dassi handles the repetition. Pause, resume, or delete your workflows anytime on app.dassi.ai → Your Workflows.
