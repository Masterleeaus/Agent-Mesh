# Titan Quality & Compliance Team — Final Contract

The team exists to separate operational activity from verified quality/compliance outcomes.

It may inspect service quality, triage complaints, review evidence, detect compliance exceptions and prepare corrective work. Low-risk actions may execute only with granted capability. Medium/high/critical actions require approval. Every material lifecycle transition is company-scoped and auditable.

Primary outcome measures:
- quality failures detected and resolved
- re-cleans completed and verified
- complaints resolved
- incidents investigated and closed
- corrective actions completed
- audit integrity verified
- compliance exceptions resolved
- customer satisfaction/review recovery
- prevented repeat failures

Workforce integration is declared in `manifests/workforce.json`.
