Meet the Night Shift: The 11 Agents Running Your Lawn Care Back Office While You Sleep

JG
Justin Grant
Jun 23, 2026 · 5 min read

Most software makes you do more work to get value out of it. You log in, you click around, you configure dashboards you'll never open again. Drafted works the other way: it does the work, and you wake up to the results.

Here's what's actually happening overnight while your trucks are in the yard and you're off the clock.

The agents, in plain English

1. The Quote Drafter. Pulls every new lead and the job details, then writes a ready-to-send quote using your pricing. Mornings become "review and send," not "build from zero."

2. The Lead Responder. Drafts a real reply to every inbound inquiry overnight—not a canned auto-reply, an actual answer to what the homeowner asked. You approve, it goes.

3. The Route Planner. Looks at tomorrow's stops and sequences them so your crew isn't crisscrossing the county burning fuel and daylight.

4. The Margin Watchdog. Flags jobs where your numbers are slipping—a property that's eating more hours than it's billing, materials creeping past estimate. You see the leak before it becomes a pattern.

5. The Upsell Spotter. Reviews active jobs and points out the obvious add-ons—the customer on a mowing plan whose beds are overgrown, the property that's due for aeration. Revenue you already earned the right to ask for.

6. The Follow-Up Agent. Chases the quotes that went quiet. The estimate you sent Tuesday that got no reply by Friday gets a nudge drafted for you.

7. The Daily Report Writer. Summarizes yesterday—jobs done, revenue, what's pending—so you start the day knowing where you stand without digging through five apps.

8. The Schedule Reconciler. Catches the conflicts and gaps before they bite—double-bookings, holes in the route, crews stacked wrong.

9. The Review Requester. Identifies the happy customers worth asking for a review and drafts the ask, so your reputation compounds without you remembering to do it.

10. The Lead Source Analyst. Tells you which channels are actually producing paying jobs versus just producing tire-kickers, so your ad money goes where it works.

11. The Pipeline Tracker. Keeps a running picture of what's quoted, what's pending, and what's likely to close, so "how's the month looking" has a real answer.

It plugs into what you already use

Drafted connects to the tools lawn and landscape crews already run—Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, CompanyCam. You don't rip out your CRM. The agents read from it and write back to it, so the work shows up where you already look.

Why "overnight" matters

Field-service work doesn't leave room for back-office work. You're on properties during business hours and recovering after. The back office gets the scraps of your attention—which is exactly why quotes go out late and upsells get missed.

Running the agents overnight flips that. The thinking work happens while you're off. The decisions—approve, send, tweak—happen in a focused 20 minutes with your coffee. You're not managing software. You're reviewing finished drafts.

You stay in the driver's seat

Nothing fires without you. Every quote, every reply, every follow-up is drafted and waiting for your yes. The agents do the heavy lifting; you keep the judgment calls. That's the whole point—you get your evenings back without handing over your business to a black box.

Drafted runs 11 AI agents overnight for lawn care and landscaping operators. Quotes, replies, routes, and margin alerts, drafted and waiting for your approval each morning.
