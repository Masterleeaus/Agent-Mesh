# Personal Zero context relationships

Issue #768 distinguishes the portable human principal (One) and their Personal Zero from company relationships. The new context contract binds one Zero manifestation to one company relationship, role, bounded data scope, capability projection, and revision while remaining authority-neutral.

Revoking a relationship increments its revision and makes the context unusable. Personal state is not deleted by this operation; company-scoped capabilities and data access are simply no longer active. Reconnect and consequential consumers must re-resolve a fresh context before use.
