# Business Reality observation and reconfiguration

Issue #767 requires incremental, provenance-bearing Reality observations and governed change proposals without letting inference rewrite business truth.

The canonical package now validates company scope, timestamps/revisions, fact provenance and confidence; compares observations deterministically; distinguishes fresh from stale observations; rejects no-op proposals; and emits semantic diffs with stable approval fingerprints. Proposals remain `PROPOSED`, authority-neutral, and approval-required. They must be routed through the existing governance/execution/evidence owners before any consequential change.
