# Titan Revenue Journey — Pass 04

Pass 04 adds a projection-only commercial-origin evidence seam around the retained booking/job lifecycle. It preserves the existing booking and Titan Field truth owners while making operational delivery traceable back to the most specific correlated commercial origin (quote, then opportunity, then lead).

The wrapper requires canonical booking/job source domains, explicit job materialization evidence, explicit completion evidence for completed jobs, the `company_id` boundary, and deterministic replay. It grants no execution authority and does not create or mutate booking/job records.
