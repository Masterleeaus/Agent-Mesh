## SECTION B — Four-Pain Agitation ("The Real Cost of Doing the Back Office Yourself")

**Eyebrow:** SOUND FAMILIAR?

**Headline:** The work that's quietly costing you jobs

**Intro line:**
You didn't start a landscaping business to spend your nights buried in quotes and follow-ups. But that's where the money leaks — not on the job site, in the hours after.

**Pain 1 — Quotes that go out too late**
The lead that came in at 7 PM doesn't get a number until two days later. By then the homeowner already hired whoever replied first. Speed wins these jobs, and the trucks parking at 6 means your business stops selling at 6.

**Pain 2 — Recurring contracts walking out the door**
The maintenance contract you lost in March isn't a March problem. It's every cut, every visit, March through November — gone to the other guy, all because one quote sat in your drafts too long.

**Pain 3 — Upsells you earned but never asked for**
The mowing customer whose beds are overgrown. The property that's due for aeration. You're already on-site, you already have the relationship — but nobody's flagging the obvious add-on, so the revenue just sits there.

**Pain 4 — Nights and weekends that aren't yours**
The back office gets the scraps of your attention, after dark, away from your family. The thinking work always lands in the exact hours you have the least left to give.

**Transition line into product (bold):**
None of this is a hustle problem. It's a throughput problem. And throughput is exactly what Drafted fixes — overnight, while you sleep.
