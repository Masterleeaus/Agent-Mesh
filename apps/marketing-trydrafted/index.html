FAQ

The questions every owner asks first.

Will my team need to learn new software? +

No. Your team keeps using your existing service-management software exactly as they do today — Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, whatever you run. Drafted reads from those tools. Your team never touches anything else.

What happens when the AI gets it wrong? +

Every output is a draft. Nothing sends to a customer without your approval. When a draft is off, you edit or dismiss it — and the agents adapt to your corrections over time.

How is this different from my CRM's built-in AI? +

Jobber, ServiceTitan, and Housecall Pro store your data and increasingly ship narrow AI features inside their products. Drafted is the operations layer on top — drafting the replies, quotes, reports, and route plans your CRM doesn't draft for you. We complement your CRM, not replace it.

My pricing is custom. Will it know my margins? +

Yes. We load your pricing rules into the agent during onboarding — labor formulas, waste markups, per-cluster pricing logic, your specific trade conventions. Tunable in one config file. As one example: Firsthand's landscape quote builder uses a 25% gross margin with 11 separate burden components configured per Ryan's calculator. The pattern works for any trade's pricing model.

Is my customer data safe? +

Drafted talks to your CRM and photo tool via official APIs with scopes you control — Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, CompanyCam, and similar. No customer data leaves your account except for the specific LLM inference calls — and those don't train any model.

What does it cost? +

Tiered by company size. Solo-operator businesses start around $400/mo. Larger businesses (10+) start around $1,500/mo. Replaces $50K–$150K of operator labor a year. Demo for an exact quote.
