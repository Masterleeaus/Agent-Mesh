Personal Services · Scope areas

Manage the system around the service.

A managed-service scope can span people, useful software and the digital work around appointments. Assessment comes first; feasibility, responsibilities and specific deliverables are agreed for each business.

Possible scope areas

Look at the whole working day.

The items below are discussion areas for an assessment. They do not represent enabled integrations or guaranteed services.

01 · Understand

Service and workflow assessment

Map enquiry handling, appointment routines, staff responsibilities, information handoffs and operating constraints.

02 · Keep useful systems

Existing tools and connections

Identify what is worth keeping and assess whether a proposed connection is feasible before it is included in an agreed scope.

03 · Fill agreed gaps

Workflows and interfaces

Define specific software or interface gaps to address. Design, implementation and any third-party dependencies require separate confirmation.

04 · Configure

Digital workforce and roles

Where included and available, define role context, responsibilities, review points and boundaries for digital workforce capabilities.

05 · Maintain

Ongoing management

Describe which systems or workflows would be monitored, maintained or reviewed, and by whom, in the written service agreement.

06 · Improve

Change and review

Set out how proposed changes, feedback and new business needs would be considered without implying a fixed response time.

Boundaries first

What stays explicit.

Clear scope helps distinguish product information from the work a managed-services engagement might cover.

  1. A

    Useful tools remain visible

    Any decision to retain, connect or replace a system follows assessment and written agreement.

  2. B

    People remain responsible

    Approvals, customer commitments and business decisions are assigned to authorised people in the agreed scope.

  3. C

    Terms are confirmed separately

    Specific deliverables, access, timing, ownership and commercial terms are not established by this preview.
