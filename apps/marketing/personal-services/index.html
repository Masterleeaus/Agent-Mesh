The day-to-day

The work around an appointment matters too.

Enquiries, schedule changes, service notes and team handovers can cross several tools and people. A managed-service assessment looks at how those tasks connect before recommending any change.

01

Enquiries

Map how a new request arrives, what context is needed and who decides the next step.

02

Appointments

Review the booking, change and cancellation journey across your current routines and tools.

03

Team handovers

Clarify how staff share service details, responsibilities and customer preferences.

04

Follow-up

Understand which reminders or follow-up tasks belong in a proposed scope and which stay with your team.

How the service is shaped

Start with what is useful. Define the gaps.

Nothing here assumes you need to replace your software or automate every task. Assessment is where fit, permissions, responsibilities and commercial terms are considered.

  1. 01

    Assess current work

    Describe your service model, systems, information flows and the parts of the day that need attention.

  2. 02

    Agree an implementation scope

    Decide what should connect, what needs a different workflow and where a person remains responsible.

  3. 03

    Review ongoing needs

    If managed support is proposed, document its boundaries, responsibilities and commercial terms separately.
