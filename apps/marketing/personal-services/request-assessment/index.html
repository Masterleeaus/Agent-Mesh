Personal Services · Request assessment

Considering an assessment? Start with a clear picture of the work.

This page explains what may be useful to prepare for an assessment request. The review site cannot receive or submit that request.

A practical preparation path

Bring context, not a polished specification.

A conversation can begin with the routines that matter and what currently makes them difficult to coordinate.

  1. 01

    Describe the service

    Note the appointment types, customer journey, staff roles and seasonal or day-to-day constraints.

  2. 02

    List the tools and handoffs

    Bring the names of systems involved and a simple description of where information moves between people.

  3. 03

    Choose the question that matters

    Identify a small set of workflow problems or opportunities to assess first.

  4. 04

    Review fit and responsibility

    Discuss feasibility, access, third-party conditions, decision points and work that should remain human-led.

  5. 05

    Decide from written terms

    If a suitable scope is proposed, review its deliverables, commercial terms, timing and responsibilities before agreeing.

Optional preparation notes

Useful context to have on hand.

Do not send confidential or customer-identifying information through this review site. No information is being requested or collected here.

  • Current appointment and service routines
  • Systems involved in the selected workflow
  • Roles that review or approve changes
  • Known constraints and third-party dependencies
  • What a useful, observable outcome would mean
  • Questions about scope, responsibilities and terms
