# Titan Zero Marketing Sites

Three standalone marketing surfaces are retained as separate sites while sharing one Titan Zero brand and one canonical product destination.

| Vertical | Site source | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cleaning | `marketing-fieldcrew` | Cleaning-business workforce and operations |
| Automotive service | `marketing-fieldops` | Dealership/service-department operations |
| Trades | `marketing-tradepilot` | Plumbing, HVAC, electrical and related trades |

All product CTAs should resolve to `https://titanzero.io/app`; assessment CTAs should resolve to `https://titanzero.io/assessment` unless a vertical-specific destination is explicitly introduced.

The donor sites remain functionally separate. They are not runtime dependencies of `apps/web`.
